# Research Log Addendum: Product Direction Refined

**Date:** 2026-10-13

## Decision
The project direction is a guided voter guide with an expandable research layer, initially focused on Colorado ballot measures.

## Primary audience
Everyday Colorado voters with little campaign-finance knowledge.

## Problem observed
TRACER provides detailed campaign-finance records and downloadable data, but it can be difficult for a non-expert to sift through the volume of information and discover meaningful patterns without knowing what to search for.

## Product hypothesis
A guided experience connecting ballot measure explanations, arguments, evidence, committees, and reported funding will make it easier for voters to investigate issues and verify information.

## Existing resources
- Colorado Blue Book: https://leg.colorado.gov/initiatives/ballot-information-booklet-blue-book
- 2026 Blue Book publication: https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2026-blue-book
- TRACER: https://tracer.sos.colorado.gov/
- TRACER resources and data downloads: https://tracer.sos.colorado.gov/PublicSite/Resources.aspx
- TRACER data file layout key: https://tracer.sos.colorado.gov/PublicSite/Resources/DownloadDataFileKey.pdf

## What existing resources already provide
The Blue Book includes measure text and title, a fair and impartial analysis, major arguments for and against, and a brief fiscal assessment. TRACER provides campaign-finance records and allows users to search, print, or extract information for further analysis. Its resources page offers CSV downloads for contributions/donations, expenditures, and loan data by reporting year.

## Implication
The product should not merely repackage the Blue Book or reproduce TRACER tables. Its differentiator should be the guided connection between the measure, arguments, supporting evidence, committees, financial activity, and original records.

## Risks and open questions
- Can issue committees be connected to a measure through explicit, verifiable evidence?
- Can imported data be reconciled with official committee summaries?
- How should amended filings, transfers, loans, refunds, and in-kind transactions be represented?
- How can we avoid treating funding as proof of motive or treating shared vendors as proof of coordination?
- Which measure offers a manageable and well-documented pilot without selecting it based on a political preference?

## Next actions
1. Audit official source materials for a candidate pilot measure.
2. Inspect the relevant TRACER downloads and field-layout key.
3. Document the committee-to-measure linking method.
4. Create one source-backed measure dossier.
5. Test a low-fidelity guided page with target users.
